Privacy policy.

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Pradakshina counts your rounds when you walk around a temple, shows temples on a map and keeps a panchangam calendar. This page explains what it collects, why, and how to have it deleted. It is written plainly on purpose.

Without an account, nothing leaves your phone

Your walks, rounds, temples and settings are stored on your phone. Counting, the map, the calendar and reminders all work offline. If you don't sign in and don't choose to share, the app sends none of your walks to us.

What is collected, and when

What is never collected

Your location while you walk or use the app, contacts, other photos on your phone, audio, and anything from other apps. The map uses your location on your phone only, to show temples near you; it is not sent to us.

Your choices

  • Help improve counting is off until you turn it on, and you can turn it off any time in Settings. Only walks shared this way are used to improve counting.
  • Public profile can be turned off in Settings.
  • You can use the app fully without signing in.

Deleting your data

In the app: Settings, then Your data, then Request deletion. From that moment nothing of yours is used for research, your profile becomes private and the app stops sending anything. We delete your data from our servers within 30 days. Your journey on your phone stays until you uninstall the app. You can also ask on the web, without the app.

Backups of our database are kept for a short time and then expire; deleted data disappears from them when they do.

Who processes the data

  • Supabase stores accounts, walks, recordings and photos.
  • Sentry receives crash reports.
  • Google and Apple handle sign-in when you choose them.
  • Temple stories come from Wikipedia and temple photos from Wikimedia Commons; the app asks them for a temple's article or picture when you open that temple, without anything about you.
  • Fonts for Indian languages are downloaded once from jsDelivr.

We don't sell your data, show ads, or share it with anyone for their own use.

Children

Pradakshina is not directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect their data.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above changes and the app will say so. Questions: hello@narnelabs.com. Developer: Narne Labs.
