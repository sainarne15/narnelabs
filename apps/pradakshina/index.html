NL-002 · AndroidPradakshina Counter: Parikrama

Walk around the temple. We keep count.

Put the phone in your pocket and begin. Each round is counted as you come back to where you started. At each round the phone gives a gentle tap, and it can say the count aloud in your language.

Features

Made for how people really walk.

01 · Counting

Every round, counted

  • 3, 11, 21, 108 or any number: set your rounds and it tells you when you're done
  • Somasutra pradakshina for Shiva temples, and small circles around a tree or tulsi
  • Tap mode when you want to count by hand
  • Keeps counting with the screen off
02 · Temples

Temples near you

  • A map of temples, with a lamp lit at every temple you've walked at
  • Photos, opening hours, directions and the temple's story from Wikipedia
  • Add your own photos and suggest fixes to temple details
03 · Calendar

Panchangam and special days

  • Tithi, nakshatra, yoga, karana, sunrise and sunset for where you are
  • Rahu kalam, Yamagandam, Gulika and Abhijit muhurta
  • Ekadashi, Pradosham, Sankashti, Amavasya, Purnima and festivals marked on the calendar
  • Reminders for the days that matter to you
04 · Journey

Your journey

  • Every walk kept on your phone: rounds, temple, date
  • Lifetime rounds and the temples you've visited
  • Share a card of today's pradakshina with family
05 · Languages

In your language

English, हिन्दी, தமிழ், తెలుగు, ಕನ್ನಡ, മലയാളം, मराठी, ગુજરાતી, বাংলা, ଓଡ଼ିଆ

06 · Privacy

Private by default

Everything works without an account, and your walks stay on your phone. Sign in only if you want a backup. You choose whether to share walks to help improve counting, and you can ask for your data to be deleted from Settings at any time.

Questions, ideas or a miscounted round?

We read every message. Tell us the temple and what happened and we'll look into it.
